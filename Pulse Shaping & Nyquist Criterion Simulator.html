Experiment 7 — Pulse shaping & the Nyquist criterion

Rectangular, sinc, raised-cosine (RC) and root-raised-cosine (RRC) pulses are built from their closed-form time-domain expressions, with removable singularities handled explicitly, and compared in time and frequency across roll-off α.

1 · Roll-off & parameters

0.50
Expected: as α grows from 0 to 1, the pulse decays faster and its sidelobes shrink in time (less sensitivity to timing jitter), while occupied bandwidth grows from the minimum 1/(2T) toward 1/T — the classic bandwidth-vs-timing trade-off. The sinc pulse (α=0 limit) is the most compact in frequency but has the slowest-decaying tails in time; the rectangular pulse is the reverse.
